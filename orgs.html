Designed around clear boundaries

Personal accounts remain personal.

Joining an organisation does not automatically transfer an individual's records or grant unrestricted access to their personal workspace.

Personal and organisation records are distinct

Each colleague retains their own Hamlet account. Personal deals and properties remain in personal collections unless the owner explicitly chooses to copy an eligible record into an organisation.

Access is governed, not assumed

Membership status, assigned roles and supported resource permissions determine access. Organisation governance and protected operations are enforced by trusted backend services and security rules.

Frequently asked questions

Organisation package: key details.

How many organisations does a subscription cover?

One organisation subscription covers one organisation workspace.

Can a person belong to more than one organisation?

Yes. A member can belong to more than one organisation, subject to the membership and access rules of each workspace.

Are personal deals automatically shared with the firm?

No. Personal records remain separate. An eligible record can be explicitly copied into an organisation as a distinct snapshot; it is not silently moved or automatically synchronised.

How is the subscription price displayed?

The current price and renewal terms are shown on Stripe's hosted checkout page before payment is confirmed.

Does organisation verification happen automatically?

No. Company registration information may be submitted for review. A registry match is evidence for review, not automatic verification or a substitute for an authorised decision.

Are all organisation features available to every member?

No. Access depends on membership status, role, team assignment and the permissions associated with each resource. Some governance and analytics features are restricted to authorised roles.
