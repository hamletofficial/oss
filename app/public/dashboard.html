Resource ownership: your Firebase user ID is recorded with the submission. The listing is therefore associated with your authenticated member profile rather than an anonymous browser session.

1. Property details

Enter the property address first. Other details and EPC data are associated with this location.

Use the property’s full address as the primary property identifier. Choose a Google Places result or non-domestic EPC record when available; confirm the postcode before saving.

Select or enter an address to preview it on Google Maps.

2. Description and marketing

Describe the property and add any existing listing details or photographs.

Upload up to 6 JPEG, PNG or WebP images, maximum 8 MB each. Clear, current building or interior photographs work best. Uploaded images appear on the public portfolio and live member listings.
