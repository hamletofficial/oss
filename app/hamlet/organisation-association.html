Copy a personal record into an organisation

This creates a separate, restricted snapshot in the selected organisation. It does not change or delete your personal record, and future edits will not sync between the two copies.

By copying, you explicitly authorise organisation owners and administrators to access the new snapshot. The copy starts restricted to you and authorised organisation administrators. Property images, documents and personal record links are not copied.
