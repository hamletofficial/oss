Critical Path Reminders

Private Slack direct messages for upcoming and overdue deal actions. Manager escalation is currently fixed to the second overdue day and is sent only to managers with current access to the deal. Reminder delivery observes the configured timezone and quiet hours.

Slack notifications require members to link their individual Slack identities using /hamlet link. A Slack workspace membership does not grant access to Hamlet records. Read the integration overview.
