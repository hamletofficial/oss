Welcome to the Members Area

Hello, Member ⭐ — welcome to your secure hamlet members area.

Your account is authenticated through Firebase, so only a signed-in member can access this area and the member services linked from it. The members area is secure and safe to use for managing your account and communications briefs.

🔒
Security without the extra hurdle

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is an optional additional layer of protection. You do not need to upgrade to 2FA to use the Members Area, send a brief, or manage your submissions. If you want to review or change your security and account preferences, you can use Account Settings.

1

Send a brief to Communications

Use Media Relations / Requests to send a concise brief to our communications team.

  1. Open requests from the button below or the Members navigation.
  2. Enter the organisation and press-officer contact details.
  3. Add the headline, announcement type, press-release link, summary, and any useful notes such as audience, spokesperson availability or holding lines.
  4. Review the information and select Send Brief. Your submission is saved for the communications desk to review and follow up.
📨 Send a Communications Brief
2

Manage your submissions

Use the Brief Archive to manage briefs submitted from your authenticated account.

  1. Open archive to view your submitted briefs.
  2. Use Search archive to find a particular submission.
  3. Select one or more briefs if you need to delete them, then choose Delete Selected.
  4. Use Refresh to reload your archive, Create New to start another brief, or Print when you need a record.
🗂️ Manage My Submissions

Keep your member account up to date

Connect with the community on the Social Feed

The Social Feed gives brokers a dedicated place to share professional updates and ideas beyond their property listings. Use it to start conversations, exchange perspectives, share relevant images or documents, and engage with other members of the IBR community.

  1. Visit Edit Profile first to keep your name, profile photo and professional details current, so other members can recognise you and understand your professional background.
  2. Open the Social Feed to publish a short post, add supporting media where appropriate, and join discussions through replies and likes.
  3. Use Account Settings to review your account and security preferences. Profile information helps present your professional identity; account settings are for managing your account preferences.
  4. Keep posts professional and relevant to the broker community. The feed complements Listings rather than replacing them: use listings for property opportunities and the Social Feed for wider updates and discussion.

Free at the point of use: there is no charge to use the Social Feed itself. You can share and engage with the community without paying a feed-access fee.

Hamlet improvements — 13 October 2026

Today’s updates have focused on making Hamlet more coherent, easier to navigate and visually consistent across the platform. The legacy navigation has been removed where it duplicated the main menu, reducing clutter and confusion, while the intended top navigation has been retained. The Hamlet title placement has been refined to align with the established header layout, including a correction to the title width on the Archive page, and the outdated duplicate titles have been targeted for removal.

The animated network background seen in the Members Area has been extended as the shared visual treatment for eligible HTML pages, while excluding both index pages and everything in /app/public/. Work has also addressed background opacity so page surfaces do not unnecessarily obscure the effect. The Members Area has received small usability refinements, including removing the calendar emoji from the “Open Hamlet Deal Pipeline” button. The repository’s favicon has also been standardised around /assets/images/favicon.ico across relevant site locations.

Together, these changes strengthen Hamlet’s visual identity, simplify navigation and bring the interface closer to a consistent, connected deal-workspace experience.

Hamlet release notes — Windows v1.04 and platform updates (17 October 2026)

Hamlet for Windows v1.04.04 is the current stable release. The installer is hamlet-Setup-1.4.2.exe (approximately 89.2 MiB). The official Cloudflare R2 release also provides a matching SHA-256 checksum file so you can verify the download before running it. Release packaging checks the installer build and filename and calculates the checksum.

Property features and safeguards: residential property listings and residential resource management have been added. Public views show published listings, while Firestore rules restrict access to published records and authorised owners. Residential listings are tracked in the super-administrator dashboard, and listings show the saved publisher name and profile photo. The residential pages' profile headers have been aligned with the site's saved-profile behaviour, alongside form-spacing and back-button contrast improvements.

Navigation and consistency: the Pricing and Support buttons have been removed from the shared navigation to reduce horizontal overflow, and the separate Pricing button has been removed from the Sector Coverage page header. The Pricing page itself and the external Hamlet Support Centre remain available at their existing destinations. Recent work has also covered member/profile presentation, favicon consistency, legal-page updates and product documentation.

Download and support: visit Hamlet for Windows downloads for the official v1.04.04 installer, checksum and installation guidance. For assistance, visit the Hamlet Support Centre.

Broker Rankings — recognise achievement and inspire progress

Hamlet is introducing Broker Rankings to celebrate professional achievement and encourage healthy competition across the broker community. The rankings are designed to recognise brokers' results by comparing their average completed deal value, using qualifying closed deals recorded in Hamlet.

By making performance easier to see, the feature aims to motivate members to build strong pipelines, progress opportunities through to completion and learn from the success of their peers. It is intended to reward achievement through visibility and recognition, while giving brokers an additional reason to keep their deal records accurate and up to date.

Rankings are one measure of deal performance, not a complete assessment of a broker's value or professionalism. Results depend on qualifying completed deals recorded in Hamlet, and the public rankings are intended for brokers whose profiles are public. The feature does not promise a cash prize or other material reward.
