Introducing Hamlet Connect: a clearer critical path, right inside Slack

Property deals move through conversations, decisions and deadlines. Hamlet Connect brings the critical path closer to where teams communicate — linking an active Hamlet organisation with Slack and delivering private, relevant reminders to connected team members.

Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited today announces Hamlet Connect, a new Slack integration for Hamlet Organisations. Designed to help property teams keep important deal actions visible without adding another place to manage work, Hamlet Connect connects an organisation's Hamlet workspace to its Slack workspace through an administrator-authorised OAuth installation.

Rather than turning Slack into another property database, Hamlet Connect has a focused purpose: help teams stay aware of upcoming and overdue critical-path actions, and make it straightforward for authorised members to connect their Slack identity to their verified Hamlet account. The result is a more joined-up workflow between deal management in Hamlet and day-to-day team communication in Slack.

One authorised connection, managed by the organisation

Hamlet Connect starts with the organisation, not an individual workaround. An active organisation owner or administrator initiates the installation from Hamlet and authorises the requested Slack permissions. The integration is available to organisations with an active Hamlet subscription or trial entitlement, and workspace connections are managed through the organisation's administrative controls.

The OAuth installation flow validates a short-lived, one-time state value before exchanging the authorisation code. Slack workspace credentials are encrypted for server-side storage; they are not exposed as browser-accessible organisation settings. Slack requests are authenticated using Slack's signing mechanism, with timestamp checks and replay protection.

Meet the Hamlet Connect components

01 · Organisation-authorised Slack installation

Connect a Slack workspace to an active Hamlet organisation through Slack's standard OAuth authorisation journey. Only an active organisation owner or administrator can initiate the connection, and the organisation subscription entitlement is checked as part of the process. A workspace already connected to another Hamlet organisation cannot simply be reassigned over that existing connection.

02 · Verified account linking

Each team member links their Slack identity to their own verified Hamlet account using /hamlet link. Hamlet returns a one-time link that expires after ten minutes and can be used once. The member signs in to Hamlet, completes the account-linking step and returns to Slack with their identity connected to the organisation's authorised workspace.

03 · Simple Slack slash commands

Two practical commands make the connection easy to use. Run /hamlet link to create or renew your one-time account-linking journey. Run /hamlet status to check whether your Slack identity is linked and whether your Hamlet organisation access remains active. The commands provide private responses rather than broadcasting account-link details to a channel.

04 · Private critical-path reminders

Hamlet checks open deal actions and sends private Slack direct messages to linked action owners when a deadline is approaching or an action becomes overdue. The scheduled reminder process runs every 15 minutes. Reminders identify the action, deal and due time, and point the recipient back to the authorised Hamlet deal workspace — keeping the source record and workflow in Hamlet.

05 · Organisation-controlled reminder settings

Administrators can manage reminder preferences, including the lead time before a due action, the applicable time zone and quiet hours. The integration defaults to a 24-hour reminder lead and a quiet-hours window of 21:00–08:00 in the Europe/London time zone unless an administrator changes the settings. These controls help teams align reminders with their working patterns.

06 · Optional manager escalation

For organisations that enable it, a second-day overdue action can be escalated privately to eligible organisation managers, administrators and owners. Escalation is configurable and is not enabled by default. This gives teams an additional oversight mechanism without turning every reminder into a broadcast or removing responsibility from the action owner.

07 · Access checks and lifecycle controls

Before a reminder is sent, Hamlet rechecks the current organisation, subscription, membership, deal visibility, action status, deadline and account link. Duplicate-delivery controls help prevent the same reminder stage from being sent repeatedly. When the Slack app is uninstalled, Hamlet marks the connection as disconnected, removes the stored encrypted token material and revokes the workspace's member links.

Built around the work, not the noise.
Hamlet Connect is designed to keep a deal's next important action in view, while leaving property records, permissions and deal progression anchored in Hamlet.
Gerard Pambu, Chief Operating Officer at Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited, said:
“Hamlet Connect is about making the next important action easier to see and easier to act on. Property teams should not have to chase updates across disconnected tools or rely on someone remembering to check a pipeline. By bringing private, well-timed reminders into Slack — while keeping permissions and deal records in Hamlet — we are giving organisations a practical extension to the way they already work. It is a focused integration: less noise, clearer accountability and a more connected route from conversation to completion.”

A practical extension of Hamlet Organisations

Hamlet is being developed as an integrated commercial and residential property workspace: a connected environment for property information, broker collaboration, organisation membership and deal progression. Hamlet Connect extends that approach into Slack, giving subscribed organisation teams a more convenient way to keep critical actions in view during their working day.

The integration is deliberately organisation-led. It respects the difference between a person's individual Hamlet account and the organisation they work with, and it uses current membership and access rights when determining whether a Slack identity can receive a reminder. Slack is a companion communication surface; it does not replace Hamlet's underlying deal records or permissions model.

Availability

Hamlet Connect is intended for active Hamlet Organisations subscriptions. An authorised owner or administrator can start the Slack installation from the organisation workspace, approve the requested Slack permissions and configure reminder preferences. Team members then link their own verified Hamlet accounts using the in-Slack command.

Slack workspace installations are currently designed for standard workspaces; enterprise-wide Slack installations are not enabled in this initial release. Reminder delivery depends on an active organisation, subscription, workspace connection, member link, eligible deal access and the relevant reminder settings.
