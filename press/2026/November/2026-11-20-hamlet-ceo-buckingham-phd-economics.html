Company announcement · 20 November 2026

Hamlet CEO accepted to PhD in Economics at the University of Buckingham

Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki will join the Department of Economics and International Studies and the Vinson Centre for the Public Understanding of Economics and Entrepreneurship for a three-year doctoral programme beginning in January 2027.

Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited, the company behind Hamlet, announces that its Chief Executive Officer, Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki, has been accepted to study for a PhD in Economics at the University of Buckingham. He will be a doctoral student in the University's Department of Economics and International Studies and at the Vinson Centre for the Public Understanding of Economics and Entrepreneurship.

The programme is expected to run for three years, commencing in January 2027 and concluding in December 2029. His doctoral supervision will be provided by Professor Ali Kabiri of the University's Department of Economics.

Research connecting social justice and industrial relations

The proposed doctoral thesis will develop from Mr Mayaki's prior MSc thesis, extending its engagement with social justice, actor-network theory and industrial relations. The research will examine these themes within an economics doctoral framework, with the intention of contributing to critical discussion of institutions, work and the relationships through which economic and industrial life is organised.

The programme represents an opportunity to pursue sustained academic research alongside his executive responsibilities at Hamlet. The announcement concerns his admission and intended programme of study; the specific scope and final formulation of the doctoral thesis will develop through the University's research and supervision process.

Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki, CEO of Hamlet, said:
“I am genuinely excited to be joining the University of Buckingham and the Vinson Centre for this next stage of my academic journey. A PhD is an opportunity to challenge assumptions, deepen the questions that began in my earlier research and contribute meaningfully to how we understand work, institutions and social justice. I am particularly looking forward to learning from Professor Ali Kabiri and the wider academic community. I hope this journey will be an encouraging example of what sustained curiosity and commitment can make possible: ambitious professional work and serious academic enquiry can strengthen one another.”

Programme and funding

The expected programme period is January 2027 to December 2029. Course funding is to be provided through a doctoral postgraduate loan from Student Finance England, issued by the Student Loans Company. This statement describes the funding arrangement for the programme and should not be read as an endorsement or funding commitment by the University of Buckingham.

Media enquiries

For media enquiries concerning the research of Hamlet's CEO during the programme, interested parties should contact the Department of Economics and International Studies through the University of Buckingham website.

A spokesperson from the Department of Economics and International Studies was unavailable for comment at the time of publication.
