Hamlet to attend Microsoft Ignite 2026 virtually

Fresh off the back of GitHub Universe, and as a newly onboarded Microsoft Azure Artifact Signing client, the Hamlet team confirms virtual attendance at Microsoft Ignite 2026.

Hamlet confirms that its team will be in virtual attendance at Microsoft Ignite 2026, the four-day technology conference showcased by Microsoft in San Francisco from 17–20 November 2026.

Fresh off the back of GitHub Universe, and as a newly onboarded Microsoft Azure Artifact Signing client, the Hamlet team will join the event online. Virtual attendance enables the team to follow the conference programme remotely and engage with the technology developments and conversations shared during the event.

The announcement follows a period of continued development for Hamlet, the integrated commercial and residential property workspace. Recent engagement with the GitHub ecosystem and onboarding to Microsoft Azure Artifact Signing form part of the team’s wider focus on software development, release integrity and the trusted distribution of digital products.

Microsoft Ignite is a four-day technology conference presented by Microsoft in San Francisco. Hamlet’s participation for 2026 will be virtual rather than in person.
