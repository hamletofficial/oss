Impact in Business Recruitment Group appoints Gerard Pambu as Non-Executive Director

A leadership appointment supporting the group's operations and its continued work connecting businesses with frontier technology.

LONDON, England — Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited has announced the appointment of Mr. Gerard Pambu as a Non-Executive Director. In this Board-level role, Pambu will contribute independent perspective, strategic guidance and constructive challenge, supporting effective governance and the organisation's long-term direction as it advances its digital products, services and business goals.

Gerard Pambu brings a broad range of experience and knowledge to his work. His perspective will support the Board's deliberations, encourage considered decision-making and contribute to effective oversight of the organisation's strategic priorities.

“I am pleased to be joining Impact in Business Recruitment Group at an important stage in its development. I look forward to working with colleagues and partners to strengthen our operational foundations, support our people and help deliver the organisation's priorities with clarity, consistency and purpose.”
— Mr. Gerard Pambu, Non-Executive Director

About Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited
Impact in Business Recruitment Group Limited develops services and digital products designed to connect businesses with talent and support its wider business objectives.
